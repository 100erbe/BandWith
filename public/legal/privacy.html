In short. BandWith helps bands plan gigs, rehearsals, quotes and setlists. We only use your data to run the app for you and your bands. We don't sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. You can export your data or delete your account at any time from Settings.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is the operator of BandWith: Aria, by Gianluca Boccia & Antonio Iandolo, Delaware, United States. For any privacy request, use the tools in the app described in section 7 (export, correction, deletion) or see Help & support.

If you are in the European Economic Area, this policy is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. What data we collect

Data you give us

  • Account: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), optional phone number, instrument or role, profile photo and bio.
  • Band data: band name, logo, description, genre, website, members and roles, pricing and payment details you enter for quotes (such as an IBAN shown on your PDFs).
  • Events and planning: gigs, rehearsals and personal gigs (dates, times, venues, fees, notes), setlists and songs, availability polls and votes, RSVPs, tasks, contracts and riders, inventory.
  • Client data you enter: names, emails, phone numbers and companies of your clients, used to prepare quotes and contracts. You are responsible for having a valid reason to store them.
  • Messages: chat messages you send to your band or event chats.
  • Invitations: email addresses of people you invite to a band.

Data collected while you use the app

  • Device and push tokens, so we can send you notifications you enabled.
  • Approximate location, only if you allow it, to estimate travel distance and show the weather for a venue. It is used on request and not tracked in the background.
  • Contacts, only if you choose "Import from contacts" while inviting bandmates: the system picker returns just the contacts you select, and only their name and email are used for the invite.
  • Technical logs kept by our hosting providers for security and troubleshooting (for example IP address and error logs), retained for a short period.

4. Who can see your data

  • Your bands: members of a band see the band's events, setlists, chats and the profile details you share with them (name, photo, instrument). Fees and quotes are visible according to your role; admins see band-wide finances.
  • Personal gigs are private to you. You can also choose to hide their details from your bands in Settings › Privacy & account.
  • Clients see only what you send them (for example a quote or contract PDF).

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and band data: while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, personal gigs and messages are deleted right away. Bands where you were the only member are deleted with it. In bands that continue without you, events stay with the band, without your name.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days; technical logs are kept for up to 30 days.
  • Pending invitations expire automatically.

7. Your rights and how to delete your account

You can access, correct, export, restrict or delete your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time.

  • Export: Settings › Privacy & account › Export my data (JSON file).
  • Delete your account: Settings › Privacy & account › Delete account. If you are the only admin of a band with other members, first make another member admin. If you can no longer sign in, reset your password from the sign-in screen first (Forgot password?).
  • Notifications and location: turn them off in Settings or in your device's system settings.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. In Italy, this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting provider. Access is limited by row-level security rules, so each person only reaches the data of their own account and bands. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will notify you and the authority as the law requires.

9. Children

BandWith is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
