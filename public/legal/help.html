Signing in

  • Forgot your password: on the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and follow the link we email you. The link works for one hour.
  • Confirm your email: a new account may need its address confirmed first. Open the link in the email on the device where you created the account, then sign in.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint: after signing in with your password the app offers quick sign-in. If it stops working, sign in with your password once to turn it back on.
  • App lock: on iPhone, iPad and Android the app asks for Face ID, fingerprint or your device passcode when it starts. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & account.

Joining a band

  • Open the invite link from the email your band admin sent, or tap I have an invite code on the welcome screen and paste it.
  • No code? Choose Find my invite by email, then create your account or sign in with the address the invite was sent to: the invite appears there.
  • Invite not found, or expired? Ask your band admin to send it again.

Using BandWith offline

Your bands, gigs and setlists stay available without a connection. Changes you make offline are sent when the connection comes back.

Your data

  • Export: Settings › Privacy & account › Export my data (JSON file).
  • Delete your account: Settings › Privacy & account › Delete account. If you are the only admin of a band with other members, first make another member admin.
  • Language: Settings › Language.
